In the 13-month calendar, every date falls on the same weekday all year long!

Layout follows your 13_Sol document: weeks run Monday to Sunday and every month has four weeks. A 13th month, Sol, sits between June and July. Year Day follows December 28 and, in leap years, Leap Day follows June 28; both belong to no weekday or month.
